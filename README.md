# TIME-SERIES-ANALYSIS-OF-THE-NIGERIA-POPULATION-DENSITY

The study finds that Nigeria's population density is a very structural demographic trend, which will continue to grow because of high fertility and low mortality rates and will likely continue to increase at least till 2030. It suggests that the new national census should be prioritized, that the trend of density increase should be considered in infrastructure and land-use planning, and that family planning and reproductive health policies.
